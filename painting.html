The Colours of Light

MEDIUM: Oil on Canvas
YEAR: 2023

The Colors of Light frames sorrow not as the absence of light, but as its paradoxical counterpart. The painting proposes a polarity in which darkness contains illumination, and grief becomes an illuminating state of consciousness, rather than a void. Sorrow here emits light that is intense, saturated, and difficult to endure, suggesting that emotional weight does not dim perception but amplifies it. The use of bright, almost neon hues intensifies this emotional reading. These colors resist melancholy neutrality, instead amplifying sorrow as an acute and visceral experience.

'Hearts like Ours'

MEDIUM: Oil on Brass metal; Colored pencils on Plexiglass
YEAR: 2024

‘Hearts like Ours’ reads as a collective confession. The painting does not depict a singular story but a universal condition: to love is to endure loss. Love here does not end with death; it lingers as memories and unrequited longing that’s imprinted in all of us, suggesting that grief is collective and timeless; that to love is to carry the dead within us.
