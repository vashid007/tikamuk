SHELL HOUSE

TYPOLOGY: RESIDENTIAL BUILDING: Tiny House

YEAR: 2020

The Shell House is a tiny home situated by the beach on Ward’s Island in Toronto. The structural system of the dwelling is designed to mimic the anatomy of a sundial shell, creating a unique circulation throughout the home and transitional spaces that offer unobstructed views of the lake.
