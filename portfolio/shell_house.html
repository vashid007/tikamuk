SHELL HOUSE

TYPOLOGY: RESIDENTIAL BUILDING: Tiny House
YEAR: 2020

The Shell House is a tiny home situated by the beach on Ward’s Island in Toronto. The structural system of the dwelling is designed to mimic the anatomy of a sundial shell, creating a unique circulation throughout the home and transitional spaces that offer unobstructed views of the lake.

A house on Ward’s Island repurposed a gangway (a movable platform or bridge used as a passageway for boarding or leaving a ship) as an accessible ramp to the home’s porch

The site study and program sketches were developed during a visit to Ward’s Island. The island’s unique characteristics and architectural language served as the inspiration for my tiny home design.
Locals often travel by bicycle on the island, as the roads are inaccessible to vehicles.
Many residents live on their boats or have converted waterborne vessels into homes and community restaurants, giving the Toronto neighborhood its distinctive architectural character.
