AVANTIKA MUKHOPADHYAY

BDes.(Hons) Environmental Design | Tikamukh1404@gmail.com

I am a graduate of OCAD University, located in Toronto, Canada, from the Environmental Design Program with a specialization in Interior Design. I work between Toronto and Dubai, UAE, in the field of autism-specialized education and rehabilitation, alongside my freelance design practice. Through my experience in design and autism care, I aim to explore the intersection of architecture, philosophy, and autism support, with the long-term goal of pursuing postgraduate studies that integrate these disciplines.

My approach to art and design embraces an experimental methodology, rooted in research as a foundation to explore innovative perspectives on design challenges. I seek to advance research and foster collaborations that support the development of innovative technologies, therapeutic approaches, and built environments grounded in trauma-informed design. With a well-rounded skill set in exterior, interior architectural, and landscape design, complemented by my expertise in design research, conceptual development, and visual presentation, I bring a multidisciplinary perspective that would contribute meaningfully to any design team.

This portfolio features selected design works that showcase my academic journey and professional experience, along with a collection of paintings developed over the years.
