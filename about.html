AVANTIKA MUKHOPADHYAY

BDes.(Hons) Environmental Design | Avantika1404@gmail.com | +971 50 709 7525

I am a graduate of OCAD University, located in Toronto, Canada, from the Environmental Design Program with a specialization in Interior Design. I currently reside in the United Arab Emirates, where I apply my skills as a Freelance designer in hopes of building an independent design practice. My approach to art and design embraces an experimental methodology, rooted in research as a foundation to explore innovative perspectives on design challenges. I seek to expand my professional experience and gain valuable knowledge in a studio environment. I would be a valuable asset to any design team as I bring a well-rounded skill set in exterior, interior architectural, and landscape design, along with expertise in design research, conceptual development, and visual presentation.

This portfolio features selected design works that showcase my academic journey and professional experience, along with a collection of paintings developed over the years.
